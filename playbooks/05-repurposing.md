# Layer 5 — Repurposing (nothing gets made once)

**Claim:** solo becomes multi-platform when every piece of source material is planned as a *family* of assets from the start. One shoot can honestly become seven surfaces. And the **sequence** matters more than the volume.

## The template (adapt surfaces to the human's platforms)

From one source shoot / one core piece:

| # | Surface | Role |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Teaching post (carousel/thread/newsletter) | **TEACH** — goes out days ahead; builds reach and warms the audience |
| 2 | The long-form piece (video/essay) | **LAND** — the main event; converts warmed audience |
| 3 | Its thumbnail/cover | From the same source material — packaging is a surface |
| 4 | Short clips (from the long-form master) | Discovery on short-form feeds |
| 5 | Announcement reel/short | **ANNOUNCE** — on launch day, points at the main piece |
| 6 | Stories/ephemeral set | Launch-day presence for existing followers |
| 7 | A physical or bonus surface (print, PDF, email) | The one nobody expects; deepens the core audience |

**TEACH → LAND → ANNOUNCE.** The teaching post runs ~5 days before the main piece and does the reach-building. The main piece lands. The announcement assets fire on launch day. Don't announce before there's something to land on, and don't land without having taught — the ordering is the engine.

## How the agent operationalizes it

1. When a new piece enters the pipeline, copy `templates/repurposing-map.md` for it and immediately add its derivative rows (same ID, suffixed: `P07-carousel`, `P07-shorts`…) with the sequence dates relative to the main piece's date.
2. Derivatives inherit from the source: the teaching post's structure can *become* the long-form script (it happened in the real system this kit comes from — the carousel became the video); clips come from the assembly's strongest segments; covers come from the shoot's best frame.
3. Everything renders through Layer 3, so the family is visibly one brand.
4. Log the family's actual dates and results in the tracking sheet — the template earns trust with receipts.

## What NOT to do

- Don't cross-post the identical asset everywhere; adapt per platform (aspect, length, caption norms) — the design system makes this cheap.
- Don't let repurposing outrun quality: a weak source makes seven weak assets. The bar lives at the source.

## Done when

The pipeline never contains a lone piece — every source row has its family and its sequence, and the human's reaction to a finished shoot is "the engine takes it from here."
